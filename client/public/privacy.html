SeDiscordle Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

SeDiscordle is a word game that runs as an Activity inside Discord. This page explains what it stores about you and why.

What we collect

When you open the game, Discord asks you to let it see your basic account info and your list of servers. From that, we save:

  • Your Discord account: your user ID, username, display name, and avatar ID.
  • Servers you play from: the server's ID and name, so we can build that server's leaderboard. We only save the server you launched the game in, not your whole server list.
  • Your games: your guesses, your times, whether you won, and your win streaks.

We don't collect your email, messages, voice, or payment info. We don't use ads or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To run the game and remember your progress for the day.
  • To show leaderboards. Your display name, streaks, and times are visible to other members of the servers you play from.

We don't sell or share your info with anyone, except the hosting service (Railway) that stores the game's database.

How long we keep it

Game results and stats stay saved so streaks and leaderboards keep working. Login sessions are deleted after 30 days.

Deleting your data

To have your data deleted, contact us (below) and include your Discord username. We'll remove your account info, games, and stats.

Kids

SeDiscordle is meant for people old enough to use Discord under Discord's Terms of Service.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
